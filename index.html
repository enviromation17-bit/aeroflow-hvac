The problem

Speed matters when a customer is ready to buy.

The demo focuses on the gap between receiving an inquiry and getting the right person to act on it.

Without a lead recovery system

Customer submits an inquiry after hours
Message waits in an inbox
Owner has little context when they see it
High-priority requests can be forgotten

With an AI response workflow

Inquiry is captured and normalized
AI classifies urgency and lead quality
Customer response is prepared quickly
Owner gets a concise, prioritized alert
How it works

From customer message to clear next action.

A modular n8n-style workflow keeps each stage understandable and replaceable.

📥
1. Capture

Receive and normalize the lead from a website or connected source.

🧠
2. Analyze

Extract service, intent, urgency, score and recommended action.

💬
3. Respond

Generate a useful customer-facing response without inventing prices or appointments.

🔔
4. Alert

Prepare a concise owner notification so the right lead gets attention.
